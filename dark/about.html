About us

A family business, ten years on the road

justClimb is a family-run mobile climbing wall hire business based in Pickering, North Yorkshire, proud of our reputation for professionalism, service and good value.

Our story

justClimb has been bringing the mountain to people across the UK for over a decade. What started as a small, family-run operation in Pickering has grown into one of the North of England's most trusted mobile climbing wall hire businesses.

We work with schools, youth groups, corporate clients, shows and festivals, from the Great Yorkshire Show to school fêtes, from NHS wellbeing days to scout camps in some of the most remote corners of the county.

Every event is run by our own staff, not sub-contracted out. Our climbing team are trained to get the best out of every climber, whatever their age or ability, and many have worked with us for years.

We're proud to have delivered climbing days to thousands of children and adults over the years, and even prouder of the recommendations and repeat bookings that keep coming back.

Safety, staffing and paperwork

DBS-checked staff

Every instructor is DBS-checked and trained to work safely with children and adults alike.

£5m public liability

Full cover for every event, whatever its size, so you can relax and let people climb.

Documented risk assessments

Every site gets its own risk assessment, completed and filed before we let anyone climb.

Hydraulic safety system

Patented hydraulic rams tilt the tower into position safely, in a matter of minutes.

Automatic belay

Climbers descend under complete control on an automatic belay system, no rope skills required.
