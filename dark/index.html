Who we are

Adventure that comes to you

justClimb is a family-run mobile climbing wall hire business based in Pickering, North Yorkshire. For over a decade we've brought the mountain to schools, family fun days, corporate events, shows and festivals right across the UK.

Every hire includes our 27ft mobile climbing tower, up to two DBS-checked instructors and £5m public liability insurance, everything you need for a safe, fully-supported climbing day.

We're proud of our reputation for professionalism, service and good value, trusted by clients from the North East and Teesside, through Yorkshire and Lincolnshire, down to Nottinghamshire.

The climbing wall

pure adventure

Every hire includes a 27ft mobile climbing tower with four graded faces, trailer-mounted and tilted into position in minutes using hydraulic safety rams. Climbers of every age and ability are brought back to ground level by an automatic belay system, run throughout by our own fully trained staff.
